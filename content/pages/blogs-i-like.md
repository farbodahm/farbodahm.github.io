I often find myself telling friends or colleagues about a blog post I read and liked. Then I have to go looking for it so I can share it.

So starting October 2026, I'm adding this page to my blog to make it easy to share the posts I liked. I may update it weekly or biweekly.

Note: I link to specific posts here. That doesn't necessarily mean I agree with all of the author's opinions or everything they've written.
