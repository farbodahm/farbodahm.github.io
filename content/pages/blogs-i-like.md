I often find myself telling friends or colleagues about a blog post I read and liked. Then I have to go looking for it so I can share it.

So starting October 2026, I'm adding this page to my blog to make it easy to share the posts I liked. I may update it weekly or biweekly.

Note: I link to specific posts here. That doesn't necessarily mean I agree with all of the author's opinions or everything they've written.

- A Staff Engineer's Guide to Inventing Work: https://sujithjay.com/inventing-work
- How to solve hard problems: https://opeonikute.dev/posts/how-to-solve-hard-problems
- How to keep enjoying programming in a world of LLMs: https://discourse.haskell.org/t/how-to-keep-enjoying-programming-in-a-world-of-llms/14705
- Developers Should Read the Classics Too: https://www.mtsoukalos.eu/2026/10/developers-should-read-the-classics-too/
- Inside Go's Garbage Collector: why does it take pauses? How can you write better GO to reduce GC Overhead: https://kaizakin.site/blogs/inside-go-garbage-collector
- So I guess everyone uses LLMs to think now: https://eva.ac/blog/llms/
- You can't design software you don't work on: https://www.seangoedecke.com/you-cant-design-software-you-dont-work-on/
- Broken Ownership: https://blog.alexewerlof.com/p/broken-ownership
- You build it, you own it: https://blog.alexewerlof.com/p/you-build-it-you-own-it
- The Law of Leaky Abstractions: https://www.joelonsoftware.com/2002/11/11/the-law-of-leaky-abstractions/
